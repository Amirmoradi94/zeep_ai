Welcome to BeeBlue ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Instagram shopping assistant bot service.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect the following types of information:

### 2.1 User-Provided Information
- **Instagram User ID**: We collect your Instagram user ID to provide personalized services and communicate with you.
- **Search Terms**: The keywords, phrases, and search queries you submit when looking for products.
- **Region Information**: Your geographic location to provide region-specific recommendations.
- **Feedback**: Any ratings, comments, or suggestions you provide regarding our service.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Search Activity**: We track the number of searches you perform using our service.
- **Content Interaction**: When you search by an Instagram reel or post, we store that reel or post information.
- **Usage Patterns**: How you interact with our bot, including frequency and duration of use.
